Islamic Studies 🌙 التربية الإسلامية

Animated lessons with Hudhud the Hoopoe for every question on your review sheet: spot the keyword, remember the answer, write it.

🎯 Practise

🔊 Turn the sound on (flip off silent mode): Hudhud explains in English and says every Arabic word with an Arabic voice. If the Arabic is silent on an iPhone, add an Arabic voice in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › Arabic.
🔤 Sounds shows how each Arabic word sounds, in English letters, under the Arabic.
🇬🇧 Stuck? Tap English at the top of any lesson to turn all the Arabic into English, then tap it again to switch back.
🔎 For every question, look for its keyword: it tells you which answer to write.
✍️ The exam is written by hand: after each lesson, look, cover, write and check the answers on paper.
↔️ Lessons work best with the phone sideways. A ✅ appears when you finish a lesson.
