# Islamic Studies revision sheet: transcript

**Source:** "مراجعة التربية الإسلامية للصف السابع، الفصل الدراسي الثاني 2026 / 2027" (Islamic Education review, Grade 7,
second term), Pakistan English School (Kuwait). 9 pages, typed, with the school's answers (الإجابة) under every question.
The PDF's text layer uses a broken font mapping (ق shows as م, ي as ٌ…), so everything here was typed from the page images.

How to read this file
- Every question is copied **word for word in Arabic**, then given in **English** and, for answers, in **🔤 Sounds**
  (an easy English spelling: `aa ee oo` are long vowels, `'` is the ع or ء sound, `kh gh th dh sh` as in Arabic).
- **Keyword** = the word or two that tell the student which answer belongs to the question (see the plan).
- The student writes answers **without harakat** (vowel marks), exactly as on the sheet.
- `[FIX]` = a typo on the sheet, corrected silently in the lessons (as for Maths and French).
- The sheet has no Question 1. Page 1 only lists what is learned by heart (the surah and the three hadiths), so Q1 is
  probably reciting or writing them. **Surah Al-Ma'arij is not part of this build (parent's decision).**

Answers count: 59 in all; 46 are written in Arabic (13 are ticks, crosses or underlining).

### الحديث الأول (Hadith 1) → Lesson 2 (The angels)
> عن عائشة رضي الله عنها أن رسول الله ﷺ قال : ( خلقت الملائكة من نور وخلق الجان من مارج من نار وخلق آدم مما وصف لكم ) .

`[FIX]` the sheet has عنهما; ʿĀʾisha is one person, so رضي الله عنها.
English: ʿĀʾisha (may Allah be pleased with her) reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The angels were created
from **light**, the jinn were created from a smokeless flame of **fire**, and Adam was created from what has been described
to you (**clay**)." Keywords: نور (light) · نار (fire) · آدم (Adam).

### الحديث الثاني (Hadith 2) → Lesson 6 (Sins of the tongue)
> عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه قال : قال رسول الله ﷺ : ( تجد من شر الناس يوم القيامة عند الله ذا الوجهين ، الذي يأتي هؤلاء بوجه وهؤلاء بوجه ) .

English: Abu Hurayra (may Allah be pleased with him) said: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "You will find among the worst
of people on the Day of Resurrection, in the sight of Allah, the **two-faced** person, who comes to some people with one
face and to others with another face." Keyword: ذا الوجهين (the two-faced one).

### الحديث الثالث (Hadith 3) → Lesson 5 (Prayer)
> عن أنس بن مالك رضي الله عنه أن رسول الله ﷺ قال : ( ما بال أقوام يرفعون أبصارهم إلى السماء في صلاتهم ، فاشتد قوله في ذلك حتى قال : لينتهن عن ذلك أو لتخطفن أبصارهم ) .

`[FIX]` the sheet has ابصارهم the first time; أبصارهم as in the second.
English: Anas ibn Malik (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "What is wrong with
people who **raise their eyes to the sky** in their prayer?" He spoke very strongly about it, until he said: "They must
stop that, or their sight will be snatched away." Keywords: يرفعون أبصارهم إلى السماء (raise their eyes to the sky).

### Q5 (c) عرف ما يأتى — Define the following
The three answers are made of the same building blocks: ما · يثاب (is rewarded) · يأثم (is sinful) · فاعل (the doer) ·
تارك (the one who leaves it) · ولا (and not). السنة is feminine, so its answer ends in ـها.

| # | Word | Answer | Sounds | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | الواجب (al-waajib, required) | ما يثاب فاعله ويأثم تاركه | maa yuthaabu faa'iluhu wa ya'thamu taarikuh | what its doer is rewarded for and its leaver is sinful for |
| 2 | السنة (as-sunna, recommended) | ما يثاب فاعلها ولا يأثم تاركها | maa yuthaabu faa'iluhaa wa laa ya'thamu taarikuhaa | what its doer is rewarded for, and its leaver is not sinful |
| 3 | المكروه (al-makrooh, disliked) | ما يثاب تاركه ولا يأثم فاعله | maa yuthaabu taarikuhu wa laa ya'thamu faa'iluh | what its leaver is rewarded for, and its doer is not sinful |
