🗣️ Does Professor Atom sound robotic? Make him sound more natural on iPhone and iPad

Professor Atom reads aloud with your device's own voices. iPhones and iPads come with basic voices, but Apple also has free Premium and Enhanced voices that sound far more natural. You download one once per device:

  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
  2. Tap English. Look under English (UK) for a voice marked Premium (best) or Enhanced, for example Serena, Jamie, Kate or Daniel. The list depends on your iOS version.
  3. Tap the download button (☁️ or ⬇) next to the Premium or Enhanced version. It is a large download (often a few hundred MB), so use Wi-Fi and wait until it finishes.
  4. In the same Voices → English screen, tap the new voice so it has a tick ✓. Professor Atom uses the voice ticked there.
  5. Close this site completely and open it again: swipe Safari (or the Home Screen app) away in the app switcher. Then in any lesson, tap ⚙️ (or the voice box at the bottom when the device is upright) and check the voice list says 📱 iPhone/iPad voice (from Settings). That is the default on iPhone and iPad.

Still sounds the same? Make sure the download finished (no progress circle in Settings), close the site completely and reopen it, or restart the iPhone. You can also pick a named voice from the ⚙️ list instead; the best ones are at the top, marked (Premium) or (Enhanced). The site remembers your choice.

💡 Try the 🐢 Slow or Normal speed too. Siri's own voice can't be used by websites. On a Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices, then pick the voice the same way.
